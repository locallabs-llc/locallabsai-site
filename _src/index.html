Local Labs LLC

Local Labs is a small, independent company. Today our business is The Nani Company, an Etsy shop that sells digital planners, printable calendars and spreadsheet tools for everyday budgeting.

We design the products ourselves and sell them through Etsy. We also build small software tools for our own use, such as an internal app that shares our Etsy listings on our Pinterest account. These tools run only for Local Labs and are not offered to anyone else.
